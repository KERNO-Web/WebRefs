Esports Pro

Home of the Esports Pro Circuit.

Esports Pro is a tournament operator focused on Dota 2. We run the Esports Pro Circuit, an online series that gives competitive teams regular, well-organized events to play in.

Each season is built around a clear format, a fixed schedule and published results. Groups, brackets, rosters and match details stay available long after an event ends.

We work with teams, casters and partners to keep every series on time and every result on record.
