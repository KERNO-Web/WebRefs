Season 2 playoff bracket is set

The group stage is over and eight teams continue in the double-elimination playoffs.

Group winners go straight to the Upper Bracket Semifinals. Second and third place meet in the Upper Bracket Quarterfinals, and fourth place starts in the lower bracket.

All playoff series are best-of-three except the Grand Final, which is best-of-five.
