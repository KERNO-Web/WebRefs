Season 2: how the group stage works

Ten teams are split into two groups of five. Every team plays every other team in its group once, in a best-of-three series.

A 2–0 win is worth 3 points, a 2–1 win is worth 2 points and a 1–2 loss still earns 1 point.

Standings decide the playoff route: first place enters the Upper Bracket Semifinals, second and third the Upper Bracket Quarterfinals, fourth the lower bracket. Fifth place is eliminated.
